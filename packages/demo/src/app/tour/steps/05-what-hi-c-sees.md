Hi-C measures how often pairs of sites in the genome touch, averaged over
millions of cells. A loop brings sites that are far apart along the fiber close
together, so they touch more often.

The contact map estimates that from the loops: every pair of sites, colored by
how close the loops bring them. Above the diagonal is this moment; below it, the
average over time, which is closer to what an experiment sees.

The domains appear as squares along the diagonal, and the loops held at both
boundaries of a domain as dots at its corners.

That's the tour. *Explore this setup* takes you to the full app with this model.
