A chromosome carries many extruders at once, and they get in each other's way.

A leg can't step onto a site that another leg holds, so it waits there,
**blocked**. Loops end up side by side, or nested one inside another, and in the
kymograph you can see legs stall where they meet.

Blocked extruders let go sooner than free ones, which keeps the traffic moving.
